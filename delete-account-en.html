Previous-version accounts and data deletion

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Vaktinde 2

The current version of Vaktinde uses no account and sends no data to any server. Your reminders live only on your phone; deleting the app deletes that data too.

If you signed in to the previous version

The previous version of Vaktinde let you optionally sign in with Apple or Google and use sync. You can ask for the data stored on the server under that account to be deleted at any time.

  1. If you still use the previous version: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  2. If you no longer have it or have moved to the current version: email alisahindev@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject Account deletion request. We complete the request within a reasonable time and confirm by email.

What gets deleted?

  • Your account record, email address and name.
  • Your reminders, schedules, action history, groups, tracked items and preferences stored on the server.

Deletion cannot be undone. Data on your phone is not affected.
